We all started for the usual reasons — get fitter, lose a few kilos, train for a race we signed up for after two beers

That part lasted a month, maybe two. What kept us going afterwards was something else entirely, and honestly, we're still not sure we can name it. Running just became part of who we are. Even on the weeks we hate it

We got tired of ads where every run is easy, every sky is clear and everyone finishes. Whoever those people are, they don't run with us

Love Running Hate is made for the whole thing. The mornings when everything clicks and you feel like you could go forever. And the ones where nothing clicks at all – you just get it done and go home.

If you know both, you know why the name isn't Love Running. It's the full sentence
